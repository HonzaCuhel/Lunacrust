- HyperFrames 0.8.29, five authored scenes, 35 seconds, 1920×1080 at 30 fps.

## Visual and runtime checks

`hyperframes check` passes with zero runtime, layout and contrast errors. The one
remaining lint warning is intentional: the eight-world montage uses eight
adjacent one-second clips on one track. Tight display line boxes and cropped
image wrappers carry narrow, intentional layout annotations after visual review.

Nineteen screenshots covered the first frame, held scenes, both sides of major
and internal cuts, and the final frame at 34.97 seconds. All were inspected.
A separate contact sheet from the encoded MP4 confirmed the actual delivered
frames, titles, footage and closing lockup. No black/reset tail or debug overlays
appeared in those inspected frames. Original footage stays ungraded.

The renderer initially refused a nearly full disk. Only explicit unpacked build
intermediates were removed, while final installers and source archives were
preserved. The successful render used the supported `--low-memory-mode` streaming
path, one worker, and took about 73 seconds. No preflight guard was disabled.
