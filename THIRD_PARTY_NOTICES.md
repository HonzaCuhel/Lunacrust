The soundtrack consists of `classic.wav`, `dark-cave.wav` and `explore.wav`, synthesized from original note sequences and generated waveforms by `tools/make-soundtrack.js`. The earlier prototype MP3s are not distributed. The icon is drawn from original geometry by `tools/make-icon.js`; the textures are generated by the game's source code.
