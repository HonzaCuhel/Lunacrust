# Lunacrust visual direction
Expedition chart: a large procedural destination, compact planet selector, factual environment briefing and obvious launch action. The running world remains the visual center.

Deep ink and warm ivory for low-light desktop play; chartreuse selected actions, mineral teal measurements, terracotta hazards. Self-host Space Grotesk under OFL. Crescent/navigation glyph replaces grass-cube identity. Visible focus, clear labels, immediate bounded settings and restore defaults.

Menus operate the game. Respect reduced motion, original alien silhouettes, and minimum 960x600 desktop window. Controls remain reachable through scrolling.
